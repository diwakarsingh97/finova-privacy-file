FINOVA · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

Your privacy matters. This page explains how information is handled when you use the FINOVA website and its interactive features.

Effective date: 6 October 2026

1. Introduction

FINOVA is a financial technology concept and educational platform. This Privacy Policy explains the general approach to information submitted through this website.

2. Information You Provide

You may voluntarily enter information when interacting with FINOVA, such as questions entered into the Ask FINOVA interface or information included when contacting the founder by email.

3. Ask FINOVA

Questions entered into Ask FINOVA are processed by the website's current local educational answer engine. The current single-file version does not send those questions to a FINOVA backend server.

4. Local Browser Storage

The website may use your browser's local storage to remember your Ask FINOVA conversation on the same browser and device. This information remains within your browser unless you clear your browser storage.

5. How Information Is Used

  • To provide the interactive website experience.
  • To remember local conversation state where enabled.
  • To respond to questions within Ask FINOVA.
  • To respond if you voluntarily contact the founder.

6. Information You Should Not Submit

Do not enter passwords, bank account credentials, card numbers, authentication codes, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive personal information into FINOVA's public-facing interfaces.

7. Email Contact

If you choose to contact FINOVA or its founder through the displayed email address, your email provider will process the email according to its own privacy policies.

8. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party resources such as web fonts. Those services may process technical information according to their own policies.

9. Security

Reasonable care is taken in designing the website, but no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Avoid submitting sensitive financial or authentication information.

10. Children's Privacy

FINOVA is not designed to collect personal information from children. Users should avoid submitting personal or sensitive information through public interfaces.

11. Your Choices

You can clear the Ask FINOVA conversation using the "Clear conversation" control. You can also clear the website's stored data through your browser settings.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as FINOVA develops new features or changes how information is handled. Updated versions will be published on this page.
